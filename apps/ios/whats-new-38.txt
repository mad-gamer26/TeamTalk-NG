The same layout as build 36, with clearer names and audio fixes.
- The talk button now says Unmute and Mute.
- Old names are back: Reset to Default, Send private message, Kick user, Move user, View and Edit properties, Create Channel, Sound Events, Text To Speech Events, Monitor Info.
- Audio: the app always plays along with other apps (the setting is gone); it only uses call audio while you're on a server or in Settings → Sound System; it comes back by itself after dictation or a phone call. The Monitor no longer switches to the earpiece or puts Bluetooth headphones in mono.
- Automatic gain control and noise suppression are off by default.
Fixes: admins join password channels without typing the password and can move themselves; no freezing while someone types to you; the "Set away status after" field can be tapped; the Monitor "no longer knows this phone" message; a crash when stopping an AudioNet stream into a channel.
